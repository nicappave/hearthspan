# Screenshot folders

Generated by `game/tools/shots.ps1` (docs/plan.md section 2.1). Never hand-edit these PNGs; always
regenerate with the script.

- **`current/`**: the thirteen standard shots (`01-open.png` to `13-menu.png`) as of the most recent
  run. Overwritten every time `shots.ps1` runs. During development the folder was re-committed at
  every FABLE STOP, so each phase could be compared with the one before.
- **`baseline-<date>/`**: a frozen copy of `current/` taken once, at a fixed point in the project's
  history, and never regenerated. `baseline-2026-09-03/` is the "before" picture for Plan v3: the
  state of the game before the Plan v3 usability and visual pass (docs/plan.md section 0.1), when
  the standard set was seven shots. Every later phase's shots are compared against it to prove the
  plan is actually working.
- The loose PNGs directly in this folder (`main-view.png`, `wave0-map.png`, `build-before.png`, and
  similarly named files) are pre-plan history from the July 2026 stages. They predate `shots.ps1` and
  the standard shot set, are not regenerated by anything, and are kept only as historical record.
- `baseline-2026-09-05/` is the "before" picture for Plan v4: shots of seed 954489347 (exported
  build), frozen by Fable at FABLE STOP 5 of v3.
  `01-open.png` (turn 0 prompt), `02-t49-close.png` (idle turn 49), `03-t49-hearth-h7.png` (the
  capital tile at camera height 7 with the Great Hearth complete), `04-t49-hearth-close.png`. Plan
  v4's shots 10 and 11 were taken on the same seed and compared against these (docs/plan-v4.md 2.1);
  the current shots 10 and 11 are different views, added in plan v5.
